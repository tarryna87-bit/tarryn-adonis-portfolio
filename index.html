ABOUT
01 / 04

Tarryn writes about the things people actually talk about at the kitchen table — what’s making us sick, who we’re falling for, and the neighbours doing something worth noticing.

Her reporting and features run across health and wellness, relationships and family life, and human interest, with bylines at Pregged.com, SA People, and Koala Journal. She works comfortably across registers — a service piece breaking down what the research says about ultra-processed food, a personal-toned guide to staying friends with an ex, a same-day news piece out of Cape Town.

What holds it together is the same instinct in every piece: find the human stake inside the headline, and write it so someone actually finishes reading.
