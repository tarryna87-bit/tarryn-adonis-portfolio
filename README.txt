Tarryn Adonis Writing Portfolio
================================

This is a recreated version of the portfolio based on the live Netlify site and the uploaded writing portfolio PDF.

The article titles have been preserved exactly as supplied in the portfolio, and the 21 article links were taken from the embedded hyperlinks in the PDF.

To deploy on Netlify:
1. Go to your Netlify dashboard.
2. Open the existing site.
3. Choose Deploys / Add new deploy / Deploy manually (wording may vary).
4. Upload this folder or the ZIP contents.
5. The site will use index.html as its homepage.

All article links open the original published article in a new browser tab.
